# Social Media Engagement Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes social media engagement data to uncover insights into audience behavior, content performance, and posting strategies. It leverages **sentiment analysis**, **exploratory data analysis (EDA)**, and **interactive dashboards** to provide actionable recommendations for marketers, influencers, and companies.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals
- **Measure engagement** across platforms.  
- **Identify content** that resonates with audiences.  
- **Optimize posting strategies** for better reach and impact.  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement
1. Collect and preprocess social media engagement data.  
2. Analyze popular content and posting patterns.  
3. Identify key metrics for measuring engagement.  
4. Provide recommendations to improve campaign success.  

---

## 📊 Data Collection
- Source: **Pratinik Infotech - Social Media Sentiments Analysis Dataset**  
- Includes: text, timestamps, hashtags, countries, likes, retweets.  
- Platforms analyzed: **Facebook, TikTok, Twitter, Instagram, Snapchat**.  

---

## 🔍 Data Analysis & EDA
- Verified **no missing values** – clean dataset.  
- Explored platform distribution, sentiment categories, and content types.  
- Built dashboards to visualize trends, sentiment distribution, and content performance.  

---

## 📈 Dashboards
1. **Platform Success** – Post volume & content type analysis.  
2. **User Sentiment Insights** – Sentiment by content type & region.  
3. **Content Performance** – Positive vs. negative sentiment trends.  
4. **Global Comparative Analysis** – Sentiment across major cities.  
5. **Growth Rate Analysis** – Monthly post volume & follower growth.  
6. **Top & Bottom Creators** – Performance ranking and sentiment analysis.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Snapchat & TikTok** show higher positive sentiment scores.  
- Influencers in **Tokyo, New York, and Cairo** drive strong engagement.  
- **Summer months (May–July)** align with higher positive sentiment.  
- Content types like **Memes, Games, Graphics, and Announcements** perform well globally.  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Prioritize **Snapchat and TikTok** for campaigns.  
- Collaborate with influencers in **Tokyo, New York, Cairo**.  
- Focus posting during **May–July** for maximum impact.  
- Tailor content strategies around **high-engagement formats** (Memes, Games, Graphics).
